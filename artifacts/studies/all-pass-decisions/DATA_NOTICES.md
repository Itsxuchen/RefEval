# Data and attribution

The companion contains numerical analyses and no upstream prompts, source articles, summaries, legal documents, personal rater IDs, generated answer text, provider logs or credentials.

- **Multi-IF.** Credit Meta and the Multi-IF authors for the source prompts and checker task. The original source is licensed under CC BY-NC 2.0. paired_bits.json represents new model ratings and checker evaluations of newly generated responses, with a hashed group identifier. The literal and omitted-ending columns are research transformations. Retain the upstream attribution and noncommercial terms when reusing these derivatives. [Dataset and authors](https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/Multi-IF/tree/0ab97ce0b45c7f57772e8ba2ac1616f4b00bd3aa) · [CC BY-NC 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/2.0/)
- **RuVerBench.** Credit Peng et al. and THU-KEG. Statistical outputs derived from RuVerBench retain the CC BY-NC 4.0 terms on its original contributions, including the affected cost-order and forecast rows. This study grouped observations by task, computed all-pass verdicts and applied the replay/selection procedures described here. [Release and data terms](https://github.com/THU-KEG/RuVerBench/blob/0af0256870d6322490c7ab15eb6370779b898a37/DATA_LICENSE.md) · [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)
- **JudgmentBench.** Credit Yang et al. The package supplies statistical summaries of new task definitions and auditor–scorer combinations, including weighted counts for 30 task clusters at each budget. Cite the released expert and automated rubric judgments when using these analyses. [Data card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/judgmentbench/JudgmentBench/blob/945ff52f4c63c17006dc30ec35fefbddf3ccf58d/README.md) · [Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25240v5)
- **ProfBench.** Credit NVIDIA and Wang et al. The companion supplies aggregate evaluation results, development-set counts and model error forecasts only. Obtain source data under the NVIDIA dataset terms. [ProfBench dataset and terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/ProfBench) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18941)
- **SummEval, G-Eval and UniEval.** Credit Fabbri et al., Liu et al. and Zhong et al. Source material comes from CNN/Daily Mail summarization. This companion distributes new article-group error counts, calibration choices and protocol metadata. The crossover counts use pseudonymous article numbers, per-summary expert-vector positions and recorded decisions aggregated over outputs and query orders. Positions do not identify people across summaries. The optional source replay retrieves the pinned upstream files or reads a local cache. Download original expert records and archived judge scores from the respective authors to inspect original material. [SummEval](https://github.com/Yale-LILY/SummEval) · [G-Eval](https://github.com/nlpyang/geval) · [UniEval](https://github.com/maszhongming/UniEval)

The original software in the public RefEval repository is MIT licensed. Data remain subject to the upstream terms described above.

The complete-audit controls in [full_audit_cluster_counts.csv](inputs/full_audit_cluster_counts.csv) derive from the same JudgmentBench and SummEval releases. They contain weighted event and error counts for 30 legal task clusters and 70 news article clusters per judge. Ordinal cluster labels support paired resampling without redistributing source text or expert identifiers.

## WMT23 and the matched expert comparison

The new WMT analyses use the WMT MQM first-pass human annotations, human-seeded re-annotation records and the published span-mt-metaeval machine predictions. The WMT directory carries the original source identifiers, attribution, terms and retrieval procedure. The matched expert comparison uses the same WMT, JudgmentBench and SummEval sources and the same output panels as the main study. The package distributes aggregate estimates and code; its source route obtains text and individual evaluations from their original hosts. [WMT source notices](wmt/README.md) · [JudgmentBench and SummEval sources](source_replay/README.md)
