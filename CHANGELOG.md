## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-15

Initial public research software release.

- Offline replay of seven query policies, three task orders, canonical and
  randomized ties, and base-task deletion analyses in nine cells from two families.
- Fixed 50/50 certification-plus-SRS allocation, exact finite-population intervals,
  and separately costed whole-unit sampling.
- Partial-correction and certificate-gated update diagnostics.
- Reference-link disclosure with integer feasibility bounds and an optional
  pairing diagnostic.
- Compact numeric inputs, pinned upstream provenance, scoped third-party licenses,
  immutable numerical targets, dependency pins and automated reproduction checks.

This software release accompanies a manuscript in preparation. It is not a
published paper, an arXiv submission, or a claim of peer review.
