## C. Inclusive finite-population intervals and sampling controls

Implement one shared equal-tail hypergeometric inversion with inclusive tail probability comparisons. Use SciPy away from a boundary and exact integer-combination/rational arithmetic near the requested threshold. Numerical tolerance may trigger exact comparison but must not itself expand the acceptance region. Treat decimal alpha as a rational decimal. Compare SRS, proportional stratification and pilot-based Neyman allocation for criterion-level microagreement in all nine original cells, six shares (.006,.05,.20,.50,.60,1), and seeds 1–31. The two strata are the known primary predicted label (0/1); do not use unqueried reference labels to choose allocation. All sampled labels count toward the common cap. A census is handled exactly.

Proportional sampling reserves at least one draw in each nonempty stratum when affordable, then allocates the remaining draws proportionally to stratum sizes with capacity constraints. Otherwise use an explicitly marked SRS fallback. Conditional pilot allocation starts with min(2,N_h) pilot labels per nonempty stratum and reserves one subsequent draw in every stratum not fully observed by the pilot. If this lower bound cannot be afforded, fall back to SRS. The target pilot total is max(the minimum pilot total, floor(.2 B)), capped to preserve those subsequent draws. Allocate extra pilot slots in proportion to N_h with capacity constraints, leaving at least one item in any noncensused stratum. After observing the pilot, set q_h=(pilot_correct_h+.5)/(pilot_n_h+1). Allocate subsequent samples with at least one per remaining nonempty stratum, then capacity-constrained weights R_h sqrt(q_h(1-q_h)). Waterfill real-valued allocations, floor them, and distribute leftover slots by descending fractional remainder, breaking ties by predicted label 0 then 1. Save every realized pilot and subsequent sample size.

The pilot estimator is the known pilot correct count plus the estimated correct count in each remaining stratum, divided by M. Conditional on the pilot, subsequent sampling is uniform without replacement within each remaining stratum. Sum exact hypergeometric count bounds, assigning alpha/H to each of H noncensused remaining strata (Bonferroni); add the known pilot total. This gives a conservative conditional 95% interval, rather than an asymptotic StratPPI interval. Apply the same finite-population guarantee to controls. Report bias, RMSE, observed coverage and width, plus certificates/residual disagreement from the same acquired labels. Width alone is not a claim of superiority; observe the conservatism. Any optional normal approximation must be equally labeled/applied to all compared methods.
